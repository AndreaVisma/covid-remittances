% rho = 0.90 calibrated at kappa = 1 (the fitted value); kappa and beta from outputs/shocks-simple/beta_fit.csv (16 Nov 2026).

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Ability (replaces eq. 3)
A migrant can send in a month if they are employed, which happens with probability given by the
employment rate of their occupation, $e_{o(j),t}$. In the months of the stimulus checks of January and
March 2021 ($S_t = 1$), every migrant can send:
\begin{equation}
a_{j,t} = \max\big(e_{o(j),t},\, S_t\big) .
\end{equation}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Desire (replaces eq. 4)
\begin{equation}
d_{j,t} = \operatorname{expit}\big(\alpha_{r(j)} + \delta\, M_j
  + \eta_w\, \mathrm{NoWorker}_h + \eta_o\, \mathrm{Older}_h + \beta N^*_t\big),
\end{equation}
where $r(j) \in \{c, k\}$ is the migrant's role (core or kin) and $M_j$ is the number of core migrants in
$j$'s household other than $j$ itself ($N^c_h - 1$ for a core migrant, $N^c_h$ for a kin migrant).
Core and kin migrants differ in how often they want to send ($\alpha_c$, $\alpha_k$): $\alpha_c$ describes
a household's only core migrant, $\alpha_k$ a kin migrant in a household without core migrants. With
$\delta < 0$, every migrant wants to send less often when the household has more core migrants: core
migrants share the task among themselves, and kin leave it to them.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Effective senders (replaces eq. 6)
\begin{equation}
E_{h,t} = W_{h,t} + \min\big(X_{h,t},\, R_{h,t}\big), \qquad X_{h,t} \sim \mathrm{Binomial}\big(L_{h,t}, \kappa\big),
\end{equation}
where $W_{h,t}$, $L_{h,t}$ and $R_{h,t}$ are the household's able senders, lost senders and reserve in
month $t$. Each lost sender is replaced with probability $\kappa$, and the number of replacements
cannot exceed the reserve.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Receipt (replaces eq. 7)
\begin{equation}
s_{h,t} = \operatorname{expit}\big(b_0 + b\, E_{h,t}\big) .
\end{equation}
A household with no effective sender receives in 0.6\% of months, one with a single effective sender in
96\%. The survey records receipt but not who sent, so it cannot separate how often a migrant wants to
send from how reliably their sending reaches the household. We place all differences between core and
kin migrants in desire and let every sender raise receipt equally; a model with separate coefficients for
core and kin senders fits no better (likelihood-ratio test, $p = 0.42$).

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Transfers (replaces eq. 8)
\begin{equation}
T_{h,t} = s_{h,t}\,\max\big(E_{h,t},\, 1\big), \qquad T_t = \sum_h \mathbb{E}\big[T_{h,t}\big].
\end{equation}
