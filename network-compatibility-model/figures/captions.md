## A1_network_sizes.png: Most households are linked to one or two US migrants

Households in the rebuilt population (3,405 connected MMP households, Mexico interviews 2010–19) by number of links, coloured by the closest tie: core (head, spouse or child in the US), close kin (parents, siblings, other roster relatives) or distant kin only (aggregated link).

## A2_old_vs_rebuilt.png: The rebuild removes the long tail of distant relatives

Cumulative share of links held by households up to a given network size. Old population: 1,019 households resampled with replacement until 10,000 migrants, every counted cousin, nephew and in-law a separate link (54% of links in households with 20+ links). Rebuilt: every connected household once, distant kin collapsed into one link per household.

## A3_close_vs_distant_kin.png: Receipt rises with close kin, not with the number of distant relatives

Share of MMP households (Mexico, 2010–19) that receive US remittances, with 95% Wilson intervals. Left: households without core migrants, by number of close kin in the US. Right: households whose only ties are distant relatives (uncles/aunts, cousins, nieces/nephews, in-laws), by how many are in the US. This is why each close relative is a separate link and distant relatives form one aggregated link.

## A4_occupations_by_link.png: Kin occupations mirror core occupations because they are imputed

Occupation of linked migrants by link type (last US occupation for roster migrants). Questionnaire-only links (most close kin, all distant links) copy a whole profile from a roster donor, so their occupational mix is borrowed. Differences in occupational exposure across households are therefore partly imputation noise; correlated job loss is treated as a sensitivity dimension only.

## B1_cps_employment.png: COVID hit migrant employment hard and unevenly across occupations

Monthly employment rate (employed / employed + unemployed) by occupation of current or last job, CPS, ages 18–64, Mexican origin (fallback: extract has no BPL). Raw monthly rates, smoothed (3-month centred) only where cells are small (some agriculture months). Agriculture is seasonal in every year. These rates drive migrants' monthly ability to send.
