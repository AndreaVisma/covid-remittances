* Personal remittances are computed as the sum of net compensation of employees (i.e., gross earnings of overseas   
Filipino (OF) workers with work contracts of less than one year, including all sea-based workers, less taxes, social   
contributions, and transportation and travel expenditures in their host countries), personal transfers (i.e., all current   
transfers in cash or in kind by OF workers with work contracts of one year or more as well as other household-to-household   
transfers between Filipinos who have migrated abroad and their families in the Philippines) and capital transfers   
between households (i.e., the provision of resources for capital formation purposes, such as for construction of residential   
houses, between resident and non-resident households without anything of economic value being supplied in return).  
** Remittances coursed through banks by land-based and sea-based workers
